# Governance

Business Skills uses maintainer-led, evidence-driven governance. The repository owner is the final steward for releases, scope, safety, and brand consistency.

Material changes should document the user problem, alternatives, evidence, risks, and validation. Decisions favor narrow composable skills, backward compatibility, safe defaults, and measurable behavior. Disagreement is preserved in pull-request discussion; popularity alone is not evidence of correctness.

Anyone may appeal a moderation or technical decision by opening a respectful issue that identifies the decision, new evidence, and requested remedy; sensitive appeals use GitHub's private channel. The owner publishes the rationale for public decisions and may invite an uninvolved reviewer. If the owner cannot maintain the project, they should archive it clearly or transfer stewardship to a trusted contributor with a demonstrated record; releases must never continue under ambiguous control.
