# Business Skills visual system

## Core idea

**Horizon Aperture:** noisy early signals converge through a disciplined analytical aperture and resolve into explicit evidence, scenarios, forecasts, and decisions. Curiosity comes from rigor others omit—not artificial urgency.

## Tokens

- Ink: `#050A14`
- Ivory: `#F4E8CB`
- Evidence: `#91C85B`
- Signal: `#35C8E8`
- Steel: `#7890A6`
- Card radius: 10–12 px at 1280×640
- Instrument strokes: 3–4 px, rounded caps/joins
- Safe area: at least 7%

Use dots, partial arcs, paths, probability bands, thresholds, and outcome rings. Avoid stock business icons, generic AI symbols, fake interface copy, decorative metrics, excessive glow, or urgency badges. Only the aperture may glow.
