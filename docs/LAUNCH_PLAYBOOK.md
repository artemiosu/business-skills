# Launch playbook

## Repository settings

Create public repository `artemiosu/business-skills` with description:

> Evidence-first business decision skills for AI agents—strategy, markets, forecasting, and rigorous judgment.

Homepage: leave empty until a dedicated docs site exists. Enable Issues, Discussions, private vulnerability reporting, dependency graph, Dependabot alerts, and secret scanning where available. Require CI on pull requests after the first successful workflow run.

Topics (public, maximum 20): `agent-skills`, `codex`, `chatgpt`, `ai-agents`, `business`, `strategy`, `strategic-foresight`, `market-research`, `forecasting`, `decision-making`, `entrepreneurship`, `open-source`.

Upload `.github/assets/social-preview.jpg` as the repository social preview. Pin the repository on the `artemiosu` profile.

## Ethical distribution loop

1. Publish one compelling worked case with its forecast ledger—not generic promotion.
2. Invite critique of the strongest crux and publicly resolve the forecast later.
3. Turn recurring criticism into tests and changelog entries.
4. Share concise artifacts tailored to builders, operators, investors, foresight practitioners, and AI-agent researchers.
5. Avoid purchased stars, engagement bait, mass mentions, fake benchmarks, or claims of guaranteed foresight.

Track qualified installs, completed examples, issue-to-contributor conversion, returning contributors, resolved forecasts, and calibration—not stars alone.
