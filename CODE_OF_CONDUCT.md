# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or viewpoint. We value evidence, intellectual honesty, constructive disagreement, and care for the people affected by business decisions.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and generous in critique.
- Challenge claims and methods, not people.
- Disclose relevant conflicts and uncertainty.
- Accept correction and protect private information.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, spam, or manipulation.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through a private GitHub channel to the repository owner; do not include sensitive details in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This compact policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant 2.1. The maintainers will apply remedies proportionate to impact, intent, and recurrence.
