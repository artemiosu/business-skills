# Authorship and attribution

**Creator and maintainer:** [Artemiosu](https://github.com/artemiosu)

Repository commits, releases, documentation, and announcements are published under the identity of the human account that reviews and approves them. AI tools may assist research, drafting, testing, or implementation, but they are tools—not repository owners, maintainers, commit authors, or release authors.

The project does not add an AI system as a Git co-author. Contributors remain responsible for reviewing submissions, respecting licenses, disclosing conflicts, and validating claims. Optional disclosure of material AI assistance belongs in pull-request or evaluation notes when relevant; it does not transfer authorship.

Do not invent a legal name, email address, organization, or academic identifier for the creator. Public metadata uses the verified GitHub identity `artemiosu` unless the creator explicitly supplies more information. Future third-party contributors retain authorship of their own commits.
