# Synthetic walkthrough: AI voice agents for dental practices

This compact fictional example demonstrates the artifact shape. Its facts and URLs are illustrative; it is not market research.

## Input

> Should a two-person software startup spend six weeks testing an AI phone agent for independent US dental practices? Horizon: 18 months. Pilot budget: $12,000. Maximum acceptable loss: the pilot budget.

## Before

“Voice AI is growing fast, so dental practices probably need it.” This mixes attention, capability, adoption, timing, and value capture; it has no falsifier or decision threshold.

## Horizon Scout output—abridged

**Decision contract:** run a reversible pilot only if five practices accept a paid trial, call-completion quality exceeds the declared threshold, and staff retain it for six weeks without hidden manual intervention.

**Competing hypotheses:**

1. Support: missed calls create measurable lost bookings and a narrow after-hours wedge.
2. Alternative: existing practice-management vendors bundle adequate voice automation and own distribution.
3. Counter: compliance, escalation failures, and patient trust make apparent demand fail at retained production.

**Anti-hype result:** `MONITOR`. Three evidence lanes are planned, but only one independent group is presently observed. Demo quality and vendor announcements are not adoption.

**Timing/capture crux:** the market can be real while the entrant loses—distribution access and integration burden may dominate model capability.

**Forecast:** “By the end of a six-week pilot, at least 3 of 5 practices will choose a paid continuation under the predefined price and service boundary.” Initial probability 0.42, plausible interval 0.25–0.60. Resolve from signed continuation records; no post-hoc change to the denominator.

**Action:** conduct five paid-design-partner tests capped at $12,000. Stop if fewer than three practices activate by week three or if unassisted escalation accuracy misses the safety threshold twice.

## After

The output is not “this trend will win.” It is a bounded learning decision with explicit alternatives, evidence requirements, kill criteria, and a resolvable forecast.

For a real published case, include source URLs/dates, cutoff, exclusions, conflicts/funding, complete ledger, and later resolution. Do not retrofit the case after seeing the outcome.
